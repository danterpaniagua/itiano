# Goal

Show the time spent in In Progress (IP) on each card of the **In Progress map** (`/time/report/`),
with a **Total | W-H** toggle: Total is the wall-clock time of the stay, W-H is the same stay
clipped to the viewer's WorkSchedule (working hours).

# Background

- v5.18.0 delivered the map. Each entry already carries `ip_secs` (entry to leaving IP, or to now
  when still IP), but it only appears in the destination icon tooltip.
- The report already clips segments to the owner's `WorkSchedule` (days, start/end time,
  timezone) in `DailyReportView` (`working_seconds_bounds`), used by the Labor view.
- The map deliberately ignores the report's Overlap/Exclusive and All/Labor toggles (v5.18.0
  rule 8). This toggle is local to the map and does not change that.

# Premise (rules)

1. **Time shown.** Time between entering IP and leaving it for the destination status, over the
   full stay (not clipped to the report range). Still-IP entries count up to now and read
   `so far`.
2. **Total.** Wall-clock seconds of the stay.
3. **W-H.** The same stay clipped to the viewer's WorkSchedule: only working days and the
   start/end window, in the schedule timezone. Reuses the report's existing clipping rule; no
   second implementation of working hours.
4. **Merged cards (`x N`).** Show the sum of the merged stays, in both modes.
5. **No WorkSchedule** (missing start/end time): W-H shows `—` and its button is disabled with
   a tooltip.
6. **Toggle.** Client-side only, no reload. Both values are rendered in the page. Total is the
   default. Placed in the map header next to Copy as Mermaid.
7. **Format.** Existing style: `2d 3h`, `2h 15m`, `40m`.
8. **Independent of the report toggles.** Overlap/Exclusive and All/Labor do not change the card
   time or the map.

# In Scope

- `timetracking/ip_map.py`: carry the stay's start and end per entry, sum merged stays, expose
  `total_secs`/`wh_secs` and their formatted strings on each card. The W-H calculation is passed
  in as a function by the view so the module stays pure.
- `timetracking/views.py`: pass the existing working-hours clipper to `build_ip_map`.
- `timetracking/templates/timetracking/_ip_map.html`: a `⏱` line on each card, the Total | W-H
  toggle (action bar `d-flex flex-wrap gap-2`, wraps on mobile), small inline script.
- Mobile-first: same card layout, one extra line, no horizontal scroll.
- Tests in `timetracking/tests.py` (see Validation Plan).
- Docs: `.claude/feature_timetracking_report.md` and the In Progress map paragraph in
  `timetracking/templates/timetracking/docs.html`.

# Out of Scope

- Time inside the destination state (open-ended for Blocked and Done).
- Time in the Mermaid export.
- Remembering the chosen mode between visits.
- A day-level or range-level total of card time.
- Any change to the IP bar, Gantt, Today panel, pivot or other time calculations.
- Exclusive-aware map, trail badge (still deferred from v5.18.0).

# Technical Changes

- No models or migrations.
- `build_ip_map` gains an optional `working_seconds` callable. Without it, W-H is unavailable
  (same as no schedule).

# Acceptance Criteria

- Every card shows a `⏱` time; the default mode is Total.
- Switching to W-H changes all cards at once without a reload.
- A stay entirely outside working hours shows `0m` in W-H and its full time in Total.
- A stay across a weekend or overnight shows less in W-H than in Total.
- A still-IP card reads `so far`.
- A merged `x N` card shows the sum in both modes.
- With no WorkSchedule, W-H is disabled and shows `—`.
- Toggling the report's Time or Labor controls does not change the card times.
- Works at mobile width without horizontal scroll.
