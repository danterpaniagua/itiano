## Areas for Improvement

### 1. Missing articles (`a`, `the`)

> "each jira ticket modification creates a new ticket instead updating existing one"

Should be: "...instead of updating **the** existing one."

Articles are often dropped before singular nouns. English requires them in most cases.

### 2. Missing prepositions

> "instead updating" → "instead **of** updating"

Prepositions after verbs and adjectives are fixed in English and must be memorized.

### 3. Subject-verb agreement

> "this need to be updated into a command"

Should be: "this **needs** to be updated into a command."

Third-person singular subjects require `-s` on the verb.

### 4. Capitalization

- `i` → `I` (always capitalize the first-person pronoun)
- `jira` → `Jira` (proper nouns and product names)

### 5. Contractions

> "ive created a new skill"

Should be: "**I've** created a new skill." (apostrophe required in contractions)

### 6. Spelling

> "analize" → **analyze**

### 7. Noun agreement

> "Each Actions and triggers must have changes history"

Should be: "**Actions and Triggers** must have a **change** history." (or "Each Action and Trigger must have...")

## Recommendation

Your English is functional and effective for technical work. The gaps are mostly mechanical (articles, prepositions, capitalization) rather than vocabulary or comprehension. Focusing on:

1. Always writing `I` capitalized.
2. Adding `the` / `a` before singular nouns.
3. Using `of` after "instead", "because", "part", etc.

...would bring you to a solid B2+ level quickly.
