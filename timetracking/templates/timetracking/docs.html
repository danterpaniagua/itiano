Time Tracking

Track time spent on Jira tickets. The report is derived entirely from Jira status history received via webhooks — no manual entry is required for the report.

Setup
  1. Go to Settings and set your Jira Username. It must match your display name in Jira exactly (e.g. John Smith).
  2. Set your work schedule (days and hours) so the system knows your availability. This also drives the working-hours totals in the Time by Status panel below.
Active Tickets Panel

The Time Tracking home page shows all your Jira tickets currently in In Progress status. This panel updates automatically as Jira webhooks arrive.

Time Report

Time mode: the Overlap | Exclusive toggle in the header changes how In Progress time is counted. Overlap counts every In Progress ticket on its own. Exclusive (default) lets only one ticket count at a time: the one that entered In Progress last. When it leaves In Progress, the previous one resumes. A parent never counts while one of its own subtasks is In Progress. Paused tickets show paused in the In Progress panel. Only In Progress time is affected.

In Progress time across the range start: an In Progress stay counts for the part that falls inside the selected range, even if it began before it (for example a ticket you left in progress overnight and closed the next morning). A stay that ended before the range starts counts nothing. If you leave a ticket in progress overnight, the hours before your first working hour (and the whole day on a non-working day) do not count; the time restarts at the start of your working day. The same window is used by the status table, the Done list and Time by Project / Environment, so a ticket shows the same In Progress time in all of them. In Overlap mode, Time by Project / Environment counts a parent and its subtask once: the parent only gets the time it was in progress while none of its subtasks was.

In Progress map: its own page, reached from the In Progress Map button of the Time Report (/time/map/). Pick Today, Week, Month or a custom range typed as dd/mmm/yyyy (for example 23/Oct/2026), and optionally filter by tag.

Exclusive chain (default view): day by day, newest day first, the tickets that held your time, in the order the focus moved. Only one ticket owns the time at each moment: the one that entered In Progress last; when it leaves, the previous one resumes, and a parent yields to its own subtask. Each card is one stay: ticket ID (opens its timeline), title, how the stay started and where it went, the start and end time, the time it owned and its tags. The Total | W-H toggle switches all cards between wall-clock time and time inside your working schedule. Badges: ▶ since for a ticket still in progress from a previous day (it restarts at your first working hour), ↩ resumed when the focus comes back, ⏸ paused when another ticket took it. Returns shorter than a minute are folded into the card they belong to; their time still counts.

Parents and subtasks on the map: a subtask that runs while its parent is also In Progress sits inside a blue parent envelope (1 of 2 subtasks shows how many subtasks were in progress); the same family appears again marked (continued) when it comes back later. A parent that owns the time alone is a plain card. A subtask that runs while its parent is not In Progress is a plain card marked subtask of X, with a ⚠ parent not In Progress warning when the parent is yours; a banner at the top lists those stays for the range. This never changes the time: the subtask counts as usual and the parent gets none, so put the parent In Progress if you want its tags to count.

Overlap view: the entries map: every time a ticket was put into In Progress, as per-day chains (consecutive re-entries are shown once with ×N), with the origin and destination icons, the card time and Copy as Mermaid. When you move a subtask to In Progress, its parent (which Jira usually moves too) is not listed. Tickets already in progress from a previous day do not appear in this view.

The Report covers a date range (Today / Week / Month, or a custom range) and can be filtered by tag and Jira status. It can auto-refresh (Off / 1m / 5m / 10m / 15m) and includes:

  • In Progress — tickets currently in progress, with a live duration and zoomable time bars (WH-now / WH / 10h / Day-Week-Month depending on the selected range).
  • Closed Tickets — tickets closed within the selected range, grouped by parent/subtask.
  • Time by Status — total time accumulated in each Jira status. Flags a status as stuck when a ticket has sat there too long, shows a trend arrow versus the previous equivalent range, and calls out the oldest ticket currently in each status (in working days, per your work schedule).
  • Time by Project / Time by Environment — time grouped by tag, split into these two views.
  • Timeline — a Gantt-style chart of each ticket's status segments over the selected range, zoomable.
  • Status History — pivot table of your tickets vs. all statuses, showing time in each cell. Ongoing durations are marked with a now badge.

Tickets marked as deleted are excluded from all report sections.

Manual Time Log

You can also log time manually against any Jira ticket. Entries appear on the Jira ticket detail page and in the Time Tracking list. Manual entries are separate from the status-history report.

Deleted Tickets

If a Jira ticket is deleted on the Jira side, staff can mark it as deleted in Itiano via the trash button on the ticket detail page. Deleted tickets are hidden from the active panel and report but remain visible in the Jira ticket list with a Deleted badge.
