Time Tracking

Log the time you spend working on Jira tickets. Each user's entries are private.

Logging time
  • Use Log Time from the top nav or directly from a Jira ticket's detail page.
  • Enter the ticket, date, minutes spent, and a brief description of the activity.
Report
  • The Daily Report groups all your entries by date and shows a total per day.
  • Access it from the Time Tracking list page or the navbar.
Ticket integration

Each Jira ticket detail page shows your time entries for that ticket and a quick-log form.
